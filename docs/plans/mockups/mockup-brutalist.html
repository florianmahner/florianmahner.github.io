About

I'm a PhD candidate working at the intersection of machine learning and cognitive science. My research investigates how both humans and deep neural networks build internal representations of the visual world — and what we can learn when those representations align (or diverge).

Based at Goethe University Frankfurt and the Ernst Strungmann Institute, I develop computational tools and models that make it possible to compare the representational geometry of biological and artificial neural networks at scale.

I'm a strong advocate for open-source science. My toolkits — including thingsvision and VICE — are used by researchers worldwide to extract, analyze, and compare neural network representations.
